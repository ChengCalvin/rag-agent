# Customer Data Handling Guide

This guide tells staff how to treat customer information. All figures are synthetic.

## Need to know

Look up a customer's order only when you are helping with that customer's request. Order information is limited to your own store unless your role grants wider access.

## What to share

Share order status, expected delivery and ordered items. Do not read out or send a customer's full address or email address. Confirm details by asking the customer to state them, rather than reading them back.

## Reporting a mistake

If you shared customer information with the wrong person, tell your manager the same day.
