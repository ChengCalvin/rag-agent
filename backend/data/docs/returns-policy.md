# Returns and Refunds Policy

This document applies to all NovaMart stores. All figures are synthetic.

## General return window

A customer may return most items within 30 days of the delivery date. The window is counted from the day the order was delivered, not the day it was placed. The item must be returned in a condition that is not used or customer-damaged. A refund goes back to the original payment method.

## Category exception: electronics

Electronics have a shorter window of 14 days from the delivery date. This applies to opened and unopened electronics alike. An electronics item delivered 20 days ago is outside its window even though the general 30-day window has not passed. Staff must apply the electronics window, not the general one, whenever the item is in the electronics category.

## Items that cannot be returned

Gift cards, perishable food and custom orders cannot be returned. No exception is available at store level.

## Refund method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return being received. Cash refunds are not offered for card purchases.
