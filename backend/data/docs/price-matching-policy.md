# Price Matching Policy

This document covers price matching against other retailers. All figures are synthetic.

## When NovaMart matches a price

NovaMart matches the current advertised price of an identical item from a competing retailer that has the item in stock. The competitor price must be shown in a current flyer or on the competitor's website at the time of purchase.

## What is not matched

Clearance sales, marketplace sellers, membership-only prices and one-day flash sales are not matched.

## After purchase

A price match request is accepted up to 7 days after purchase with the receipt and proof of the competitor price.
