# Gift Card Policy

This document covers selling and redeeming NovaMart gift cards. All figures are synthetic.

## Selling gift cards

Gift cards are sold in amounts from 10 to 500. They are activated at the till and are not refundable once sold.

## Redeeming gift cards

A gift card is redeemed at the till against any purchase. A remaining balance stays on the card. Lost or stolen cards cannot be replaced unless the customer has the card number.

## Expiry

Gift cards do not expire.
