# Store Operations Guide

This guide covers day-to-day store operations and where to send questions the other documents do not answer. All figures are synthetic.

## Opening and closing

Stores open at 09:00 and close at 21:00 on weekdays, and 10:00 to 19:00 on weekends. The opening manager disarms the alarm and counts the till float. The closing manager reconciles the till and secures the cash room.

## Who to contact when the guides are silent

If a question is not answered in the policy documents, do not guess. Send it to the NovaMart Store Support Desk. The contact details are in the escalation contact field of this document's header. Include the order number or product name when you write in.

## Shift handover

Hand over open customer requests in writing at the end of each shift. Note any customer who was promised a callback.
