# Product Warranty Policy

This document covers manufacturer and NovaMart warranty handling. All figures are synthetic.

## Standard warranty

Electronics carry a 12-month warranty from the delivery date. Home goods carry a 6-month warranty. Warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship, not accidental damage or wear.

## Making a warranty claim

Staff record the claim at the service desk and send the item to the repair centre. The customer receives a claim reference. A repair normally takes 10 business days.

## Warranty is not a return

A warranty claim is separate from a return. A customer outside the return window may still have a valid warranty claim.
