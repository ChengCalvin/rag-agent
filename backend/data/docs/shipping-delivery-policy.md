# Shipping and Delivery Policy

This document covers domestic home delivery and store pickup. All figures are synthetic.

## Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days from dispatch. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs an extra flat fee.

## Delayed orders

If an order shows in transit past its expected delivery date, staff may look up the order status for the customer. Staff do not promise a new delivery date. Orders more than 5 days late are referred to the Store Support Desk.

## Store pickup

Customers collect pickup orders at the service desk with photo identification. Pickup orders are held for 7 days.
